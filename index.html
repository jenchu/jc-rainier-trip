Pregnancy-Friendly Itinerary

All hikes on this trip are paved, flat, or low-elevation — specifically chosen so everyone can participate comfortably. Nisqually Vista Loop at Paradise and Rainy Lake Trail are both ranger-recommended for pregnant visitors and wheelchair users. Paradise sits at 5,400 ft elevation — drink extra water and go slowly. Consult your OB before the trip with any questions.

The centerpiece of the trip. The visual centerpiece of the North Cascades. Glacier rock flour suspended in the water turns this lake a brilliant, almost unreal turquoise. Large parking lot with restrooms and picnic tables. Spend 45–60 minutes here — it's the photo moment of the trip. The color is most vivid under midday sun, which works perfectly with your timing.

Before you go

Good to Know

🎫

Park Entry

$35/vehicle for a 7-day Mt. Rainier pass. North Cascades National Park has no entry fee. America the Beautiful annual pass ($80) covers both — worthwhile if anyone plans more park visits this year.

⛽

Gas on Day 3

Fill up in Marblemount before heading east on Hwy 20. No gas stations for 75 miles between Marblemount and Mazama. Don't skip this.

🌤️

Weather

Diablo Lake's turquoise is muted on cloudy days — aim to arrive at the overlook around midday on a clear day. Pack layers for all days. Weather changes quickly at elevation in the Cascades.

🅿️

Parking at Paradise

No timed entry required in 2026, but lots fill fast. Arrive before 9:30am on the Paradise day. After 10am on weekends, expect to wait or park along the road.

🚗

Cars

Rent 1–2 cars at SEA. All roads on this itinerary are standard paved highways — no 4WD or high clearance needed. A standard SUV is comfortable for the mountain passes.

📡

Cell Service

Cell service is limited to nonexistent inside both parks. Download offline Google Maps for the route and save this page before you go. The NPS app has offline park maps too.
